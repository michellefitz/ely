# Theme shortlist for ELY Wine

Read from the Shopify Theme Store on 1 October 2026. Prices are one-off, in USD, and include
updates. Ratings are the Theme Store's "% positive". Check them again before you recommend one.

Every theme here is an Online Store 2.0 theme. So each one supports:

- filters built from metafields (Search & Discovery app);
- a wine facts block filled from metafields, with no code;
- app blocks from the listing app.

The choice is about look, catalogue tools and support, not about those features.

## Shortlist for the demo

| Theme | Maker | Price | Rating | Why for ELY | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Prestige](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/prestige) | Maestrooo | $400 | 91% of 869 | Premium, editorial look. Closest to the polished studio style the owner likes. Mature and widely used. | Fashion-led presets. Needs restyling for wine. |
| [Symmetry](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/symmetry) | Clean Canvas | $420 | 94% of 391 | Built for medium to large catalogues with strong filtering. Best fit for 90+ wines and many filters. | Plainer look. Needs styling to feel premium. |
| [Heritage](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/heritage) | Shopify | Free | 54% of 13 | Free. Editorial storytelling, age verifier, filters. Good if the budget is tight. | New theme family with few reviews. |

## Recommendation

Demo on Prestige or Symmetry. Prestige if the pitch is "look as polished as the app".
Symmetry if the pitch is "filters and catalogue that stay accurate". Keep Heritage as the free
fallback.

Paid themes can be tried free in the store until they are published, so you can build the demo
on two of them and decide later.

## Before choosing

- List ELY's apps (tasting bookings, iziGift vouchers, delivery) and check each one works with
  the chosen theme.
- Ratings change. Recheck the Theme Store pages before you send the recommendation.
