ELY Shop Upgrade

Make the online shop accurate now, and keep it accurate with less work each time a new bottle arrives.

What we found

28 / 92wine listings show another wine's photo or a different vintage
86 / 179filter links open an empty page
5filter collections to update by hand for each new bottle, on average

The range, the tasting notes and the team's knowledge are excellent. The shop does not keep up with them, and the cause is the same for all three numbers.

Why it drifts

Today each wine fact lives in several places: in the title, in the description text, and in the hand-kept collections that drive the filters. Nothing connects them, so every change has to be made several times, and they drift apart.

The fix is to store each fact once, as a field on the product: type, country, region, grapes, vintage, farming, size. The filters, collections and wine pages all read from those fields. Change a fact once, and the shop updates everywhere.

Three parts

Part 1 · One-off3–5 days

Clean the data

  • Move the wine facts for all 92 wines into fields. A script reads today's titles, descriptions and collections. Staff confirm the few it is unsure of.
  • Fix the 28 wrong photos. Put 87 photos on one studio standard. Make studio shots for the other 5 with Nano Banana.
  • Fix the placeholder products and the typo on the home page.
  • Correct the web addresses that name the wrong wine or vintage, or still contain "copy". Add redirects so old links and Google results still work.

ELY gets: a shop that is correct today.

Part 2 · Theme5–10 days

Move to a current Shopify theme

The shop runs on "Luxywine" 1.0, a theme bought from ThemeForest, not from Shopify's Theme Store. It uses Shopify's older "vintage" theme design, from before 2021. On a vintage theme, Shopify's own filters, wine facts on product pages and app features all need custom code.

  • Filters built from the wine fields. Only values with stock show, so no empty pages.
  • A wine facts panel on every product page, filled from the fields, with no code.
  • Pages that staff can rearrange in the Shopify editor, and that load faster on phones.
  • A theme that Shopify and its maker keep up to date.

ELY gets: filters and pages that stay accurate without extra work.

Part 3 · Custom app10–15 days

A listing app inside Shopify

A small app for ELY staff. It opens in the Shopify admin and in the Shopify phone app, so there is no new login.

  1. Take one photo of the bottle.
  2. The app reads the label and fills in the wine fields. Anything it is unsure of is marked for staff to confirm.
  3. It makes a studio shot in the ELY style, then checks that the label on the new image matches the listing.
  4. It saves a draft product. Staff add the price and approve.

Once a week it also checks the whole shop and flags problems: shared photos, a missing vintage, a wine with no filters.

ELY gets: a new bottle listed in minutes, correct from the start.

Day counts are first estimates, to be confirmed after we see the store's admin and the apps it uses.

The demo

Before any work on the live shop, we build all three parts on a separate demo store:

  • about 20 real ELY wines, cleaned and on the studio standard;
  • the new theme, with working filters and wine pages;
  • the listing app, adding a new bottle live from a phone.

ELY sees the result before deciding. The live shop does not change.
